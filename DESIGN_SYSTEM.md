# DESIGN_SYSTEM.md — AuraSound Visual Design Tokens

> Visual aesthetic, dark-mode tokens, and animation standards for AuraSound.

---

## 🎨 1. Theme Philosophy: "OLED Midnight Studio"

A minimalist, distraction-free aesthetic designed to float in the macOS menu bar or behind the MacBook notch without blinding the user during late-night coding sessions.

---

## 🎚️ 3. Interactive Components
- **Haptic Sound Slider**: Custom vertical or circular volume faders with real-time waveform glow.
- **Waveform HUD**: 60 FPS real-time audio FFT visualizer showing frequency energy across bass, mid, and treble bands.
